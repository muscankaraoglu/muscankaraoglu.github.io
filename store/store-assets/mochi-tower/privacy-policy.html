Privacy Policy — Mochi Tower Climb (Mochi Kule)

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Package ID: com.muscansoftwarefactory.mochitower

Summary: Mochi Tower Climb (Mochi Kule) does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). Game progress is stored locally on your device. Third-party advertising (Google AdMob) may collect non-personal device identifiers in accordance with platform consent settings.

1. Information Stored Locally

Your level progress, rescued animals, star bank, language preference, and audio settings are saved solely on your device using local storage. This data is never uploaded to external servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mochi Tower Climb displays optional rewarded video ads (such as continuing from a checkpoint or receiving bonus stars) and periodic interstitial ads powered by Google AdMob. Google may collect device advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS with your App Tracking Transparency permission, or Android Advertising ID), coarse location, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads.

Learn more about how Google uses data in mobile apps: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Mochi Tower Climb is suitable for general audiences (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / Apple 4+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal data was inadvertently provided, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

4. Opt-Out & Consent Controls

  • iOS: Manage ad tracking at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.
