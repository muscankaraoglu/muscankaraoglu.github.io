Privacy Policy — Chromeblade

Effective Date: 2026-11-19 | Package ID: com.muscansoftwarefactory.chromeblade

Game Factory ("we", "our", or "us") built the Chromeblade mobile game as a free-to-play ad-supported app with optional in-app purchases. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you play Chromeblade on Android or iOS.

1. Information We Store Locally

Chromeblade does not require user registration, accounts, or logins. Game progress (unlocked levels, high scores, coins, diamonds, unlocked characters, cosmetic trails, upgrades, daily rewards, and audio/vibration preferences) is stored locally on your device using local app storage.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Chromeblade displays optional rewarded video ads (for extra lives or 2x coin multipliers) and periodic interstitial ads powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, subject to your App Tracking Transparency consent on iOS), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve and measure ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses data at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing & Apple StoreKit)

Optional virtual currency packs (Diamonds) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing (on Android) or Apple App Store / StoreKit (on iOS). We never receive, process, or store your credit card, bank, or billing information.

4. Offline Play

Core gameplay works fully offline without an internet connection. Advertising and optional in-app purchases require an active internet connection.

5. Children's Privacy

Chromeblade does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party ad services, please contact us and we will promptly assist.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing app storage immediately deletes all local game data. You can reset or limit personalized advertising at any time in your Android (Settings > Google > Ads) or iOS (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking) device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
